This policy explains what information the U Design website collects, why we collect it and how it is handled. It describes how the website actually works today.

## Information we collect

The website collects personal information only when you submit the consultation form. The form asks for:

- your name and company
- your email address and phone number
- your industry and the service you are interested in
- an optional budget range and message

We also record the page you submitted the form from and the time of submission, so we can understand your request in context.

The website does not ask you to create an account and does not collect payment information.

## How we use your information

We use the information you submit only to respond to your enquiry, to discuss your requirements and to follow up on the consultation you requested. We do not sell your information.

## How your information is handled

The website itself does not store form submissions. When you submit the form, your request is sent securely to U Design through the delivery services we use to receive enquiries, such as an email service and, where configured, our customer management tools. Those services process the information on our behalf.

We keep enquiry information for as long as it is needed to respond to you and to manage any resulting business relationship, unless you ask us to delete it.

## Cookies and analytics

The website does not use analytics or advertising cookies by default. If we introduce analytics in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what is collected and, where required, we will ask for your consent first.

## Security

Submissions are sent over encrypted connections. Access to enquiry information is limited to the people at U Design who need it to respond to you.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have submitted. To make a request, use the contact options on our [contact page](/contact).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The date of the latest update is shown on this page.
